# DOS Distribution Format Exporter (`dos_distro`)

Unified CLI entrypoint (`package-as freedos-distro`, `package-as msdos-distro`,
`package-as dos-distro`) synthesizing bootable FreeDOS (`1.3`, `1.4`) and MIT MS-DOS (`1.25`, `2.0`,
`4.00`) distributions into `floppy-img`, `raw`, `iso`, `qcow2`, `vmdk`, `vhd`, or `vagrant-box`
artifacts.
